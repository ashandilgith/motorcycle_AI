1. What the app is

Moto AI is a riding aid. It estimates a safe speed for curves on the road ahead and warns you with sounds. It also offers maintenance tools. It is not an autopilot, a navigation system, or a replacement for your own judgement, training, or a qualified mechanic.

2. Limits you accept

  • The app does not detect traffic, pedestrians, animals, potholes, flooding, debris or road works.
  • Map data and GPS can be wrong, late or missing. Curves may be missing or misplaced.
  • Safe speeds are estimates based on road shape and weather. Your bike, tyres, load, road surface and skill may need a lower speed.
  • Warnings need the screen on, the app open, sound on, and a working internet connection at ride start.
  • Maintenance results (sound check, tyre check, assistant) are estimates. Have anything involving brakes, tyres, fuel, steering or wiring checked by a mechanic.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Ride within speed limits and traffic laws, and to the conditions you see.
  • Mount the phone securely before riding, and don't operate it while moving.
  • Use a valid licence, registration and helmet as required by law.
