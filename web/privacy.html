  • [hosting provider]: runs our server.

Some of these providers process data outside the Philippines.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you can ask to be informed, access, correct, erase or block your data, object to processing, get a copy of it (data portability), and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Email [privacy email] to use any of these rights.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Your bike records can only be read or changed with the access key stored on your phone. Losing your phone's app data means losing that key.
