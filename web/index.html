    Before you ride

    Moto AI beeps when you're faster than the safe speed for a curve ahead. It's an aid, not an autopilot.

    • It doesn't see traffic, people, potholes, flooding or road works.
    • Maps and GPS can be wrong. A curve can be missing, or placed slightly off.
    • Safe speeds are estimates. Always ride to the road and the conditions you see.
    • Keep the screen on and the app open. On a phone browser, alerts stop when the screen turns off.
    • Don't touch the phone while riding. Mount it before you start.
    • Follow speed limits and traffic laws. You are responsible for how you ride.
