Moto AI

It beeps before the curve when you're too fast for it, with your reaction time already counted in.
Why

Motorcyclists are 53% of road deaths in the Philippines.

A blind curve taken too fast leaves no time to fix it. The warning has to come while there's still road left to brake.
Source: DPWH road-fatality figures, reported by Carmudi
How the alert works

Reads the road ahead. Warns in time.

1

Finds the curves

Your planned route (Google Maps), or the road you're on (OpenStreetMap).

2

Safe speed per curve

From how sharp it is. Lower when the road is wet; rain is detected automatically.

3

Too fast? Beeps

Distance shown = what's left after your 1.5 s reaction. Beeps repeat until you're safe.

What's in the MVP
